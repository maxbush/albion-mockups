Open days in Oxford

The Oxbridge open day service is a paid one for families applying to Oxford or Cambridge: we plan which colleges to visit and go with you. The ALBION open day is a separate event in Oxford.

Book a consultation

The Oxbridge open day service

Oxford alone has 32 undergraduate colleges. Each has its own admissions committee, its own open days and its own timetable, and academic departments run subject events on separate calendars. Cambridge works on the same collegiate principle. No family can see everything, so the real question is which colleges and which subjects to visit first.

At an open day a student can judge the academic environment, speak to admissions staff and watch demonstration interviews. We plan the visits so that each one feeds into the wider application strategy, instead of being a day out beside it.

Part of the serviceFee
Strategy and planning for a visit£350 per visit
Accompanied attendance at the open day£350 per visit

Both parts come at no extra charge with the full Oxbridge admissions programme. Fees for the programme itself are on the prices page.

The ALBION Open Day in Oxford

This is a separate event that we plan to hold in Oxford for parents and applicants. The date, programme and format have not been decided, so no booking is open for a particular day.

Once the date and programme are confirmed, we will post them on this page with a registration link. Other plans are listed under events.

If your Oxbridge timetable is already tight, do not wait for the event. You can reach the team through the contact page, or describe your situation to an adviser directly.
