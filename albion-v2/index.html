Principle

Each decision shapes the next. So the route is planned as a whole.

The school decides which subjects are on offer, the subjects narrow the choice of university, and the university decides what comes after. We plan the whole sequence.

The order is the same at every stage. The academic profile, the requirements of the school or programme and the longer-term goal come first, then a timetable of actions.

The fallback is built early, since a single offer is not a plan.

How the work runs

Analysis first, then a plan

The work runs in four steps, and the family can see them from the start. Fees are published: a university consultation with a written report starts at £350 and a full programme at £6,000, depending on the destination and the number of phases.

  1. I

    Academic assessment

    An online assessment with our teachers, past papers included, shows where the child stands against British peers, where the gaps are and what is realistic.

  2. II

    A plan with dates

    The assessment becomes a timetable: stages, exams, documents and a fallback. Each shortlist arrives with reasons for every name on it.

  3. III

    Preparation and documents

    Tutors from the same company prepare the child for ISEB, UKiset, CAT4, LNAT, TARA, TMUA and interviews, so a change to the school list reaches the lesson plan at once. The same team works on the application documents: the personal statement or motivation letter and, where needed, a portfolio.

  4. IV

    The decision

    When offers arrive, they are compared before the family replies to any school. Schools and universities decide who is admitted, so a place cannot be promised.

How ALBION differs

Four commitments we do not bend

We work inside the British system, not selling it from the outside.

  1. I

    Oxford-based

    We work from Oxford and know the British education system from the inside. The address and telephone number are on the contact page.

  2. II

    Independent

    Schools and universities pay us nothing and there are no commissions. When we say a school fits, it is our opinion, not a paid recommendation.

  3. III

    One team

    Consultants, tutors and guardians work inside one company. A child is not passed from contractor to contractor, and decisions at each stage rest on what came before.

  4. IV

    Honest about chances

    We do not promise a place: the school or university decides. Fees are published and the limits of the service are stated up front.

Which colleges to see first
