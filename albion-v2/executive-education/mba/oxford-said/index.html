MBA Admissions Consulting: Format, Selection and Strategy

MBA Oxford: Applying to the Oxford Saïd MBA

Our co-founder, Anfisa Bashkirova, took her MSc in Major Programme Management at Saïd and now takes part in the school's candidate recruitment as an alumni representative. We work from the same city as the school, and we know what a committee there tends to ask of a candidate with a long career behind them. More than ten adult clients of ALBION have completed Saïd programmes over the past year.

Discuss a Saïd application

What the committee is looking for

Two risks run through our adult cases. One file reads as a company report with the person missing. Another carries an unusual career that nobody has explained, so it reads as if the candidate does not know what they want. Both are problems of presentation, and both can be fixed before submission.

Anfisa's experience shapes how we judge the fit between a profile and a programme: management level, scale of responsibility and the purpose the degree is meant to serve. That judgement comes first, ahead of any drafting. Sometimes it ends with a recommendation to look at another school, and we would rather say so before the essays are written than after.

Her role gives insight, not influence. The committee decides, we do not try to sway it, and ALBION works for the candidate, taking no commission from schools.

Oxford University MBA requirements, dates and fees

Entry requirements, application rounds and tuition are published by Saïd and change with each intake, so you will not find figures here. A stale number in an adviser's text does more harm than none. We check the current terms against the school's own pages at the start and record them in your plan.

A typical file contains your education and management record, proof of English, a CV, essays, references and an interview. Some programmes also ask for a GMAT or GRE. Our fee depends on how much preparation is needed, and we quote it after an initial conversation.

We also track deadlines and make sure the pack reads as a single argument. If several programmes are in play, each needs its own materials, and the executive education page explains how they are counted. The method is the same for the MBA and for other Saïd programmes. The stories of our families and candidates are collected separately.

What we do not do

We do not promise a place. At some programmes in large business schools selection is below 10%, and good preparation does not change that arithmetic. And we do not quote a preparation time in advance, since it depends on whether you already have referees, English and a test result.

If you lead a team and want to study without leaving your post, read about the Oxford Executive MBA. The formats are compared on our MBA admissions page.

From practice: two profiles, one story

We worked through this on the one-year Executive Diploma at Saïd, which is not an MBA, but the approach is the same. The head of a small fashion brand was also a non-executive director at a very large company in a different industry. She had to apply once, and quickly, because few programmes start in the middle of the academic year. A profile like this is easy to read as "the person does not know what they want", and the director role left a "gap" in the CV, although in practice it is a steady commitment. We built one line around her business: after the pandemic the brand lost a partner, the company's structure and audience changed, so she needed a strong financial strategy. The director role was not hidden, but it took second place. Her English was confirmed through work experience in a foreign company and a degree in which English communication was part of the programme. She was admitted.

Questions and answers

What is the Oxford MBA cost?+

Saïd reviews tuition by intake, so Oxford University MBA fees are best taken from the school's own pages. We check them when work begins. Our own fee is quoted after the first conversation.

What are the Oxford MBA entry requirements?+

The school assesses education, management experience, English, essays, references and interview. Thresholds and dates are revised, so we verify them against the current intake and write them into your plan.

Is the Saïd MBA the MBA of the University of Oxford?+

Yes. Saïd is the business school of the University of Oxford, and the MBA is a degree of the University. Applications go through the school, which is why we work from Saïd's current pages rather than from a general university page.

Can I apply without an English certificate?+

Sometimes a programme will consider a reasoned request to waive the language requirement, but not everywhere and not every time. The school decides, and each programme has to be checked on its own terms. We find that out first, then decide whether a certificate is needed.

Is a GMAT or GRE required?+

It depends on the programme and the intake. Where a test is needed, we allow time to prepare. Where it is not, the effort goes into essays and references.

Do you represent Saïd?+

No. ALBION acts for the candidate and receives no commission from schools. Anfisa Bashkirova takes part in recruitment as an alumni representative, which does not alter the committee's decision.

How many ALBION clients have completed Saïd programmes?+

More than ten adult clients have completed Saïd programmes in the past year. We publish no other intake figures.

I run a business and have not studied for years. Is this realistic?+

Many entrepreneurs start from there. The essays and English will take more time than you expect, but a business owner's profile, well presented, can read as a strong one.

How do I know Saïd suits me better than another school?+

By setting your profile against the programme: the make-up of the cohort, the format and the aim you have for the study. We do that before any writing begins. If another school fits better, we say so.
