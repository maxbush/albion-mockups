Principle

We do not file applications one by one. We hold the whole road in mind — from the first test to the graduation gown.

In British education every decision opens or closes the next. The school decides which subjects are available at GCSE. The A-Level or IB subjects decide which university programmes become real. The university decides which master's or MBA stays within reach.

So we plan the route as a whole and revise it at every stage — together with the family and the child.

You can join at any stage: we start with an academic assessment and with wherever your family stands today.

How we work

Calmly, in order, for years ahead

A decision about British education is taken for years ahead — and the cost of a wrong step is high. So there is no haste and no off-the-shelf "packages": there is a sequence of steps the family understands at every stage.

  1. I

    Meeting & academic assessment

    A conversation with the family and an assessment of the child's current level: where they are, what comes easily, where they are drawn.

  2. II

    The route map

    A written plan years ahead: the stages, the forks, the key exams and the fallback options. The map belongs to the family and is revised as the child grows.

  3. III

    Walking it together

    Test preparation, tutors, documents, the Personal Statement, interviews — in one pair of hands and at one pace, with no handovers between contractors.

  4. IV

    Admission — and after

    We stay close after enrolment: settling in, guardianship, revising the route ahead of the next stage.

The Albion difference

Four commitments we do not bend

An English-speaking team inside the system — not an agency selling it from the outside. These are the things a large consultancy cannot copy.

  1. I

    Oxford-based

    We live and work in Oxford, street by street, registrar by registrar. When a school needs seeing today — or a child needs someone nearby — it happens today.

  2. II

    Independent

    No commissions from schools and no network quotas: when we say a school fits, it is our opinion — not a placement.

  3. III

    Direct

    You speak with the people who do the work — no sales desk, no consultant swap after signing. Questions travel without intermediaries.

  4. IV

    Confidential

    Family matters stay inside the room. Nothing about a child appears in our materials, our talks or our statistics without permission.
