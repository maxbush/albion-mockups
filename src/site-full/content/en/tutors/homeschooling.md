# Homeschooling on the British Curriculum

Home education is legal in the UK and increasingly chosen — by families between countries, by pupils whose schools could not serve a special profile, by athletes and performers whose schedules do not fit a timetable. What it needs is what a school supplies invisibly: structure, curriculum coverage, exam entry, pastoral rhythm.

## Where homeschooling is the right answer

- **A transition year** — between systems or countries, keeping the curriculum going.
- **Non-standard profiles** — gifted pupils, elite sport, or profiles a standard school cannot accommodate.
- **After a school failure** — when a school place has broken down and a bridge is needed before the next step.
