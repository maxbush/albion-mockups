# Easter Revision Courses

Easter is the last full window before the May–June exam series. For GCSE and A-Level pupils it is the moment where structured revision — three weeks, timed papers, examiner feedback — moves grades more than any other period of the year.

## What the Easter window is for

- **GCSE and A-Level** — the final consolidation before the real papers; mocks in January have shown the gaps, Easter closes them.
- **IB** — the same window serves May-session candidates.
- **International pupils** — online formats work fully; residential options exist in Oxford for those who can travel.

## The ALBION format

- Diagnostics on past papers first — the course is built from the gap, not a syllabus.
- Small groups by subject and board, or individual intensives.
- Timed practice daily, marked to the real mark schemes.
- A written position at the end: what is now solid and what the final weeks still need.
