# Revision Courses: Intensive Pre-Exam Programmes

The weeks before GCSE, A-Level and IB exams are where preparation converts into grades — or does not. Revision courses concentrate that window: structured days of subject work, timed papers and feedback, in small groups or individually.

## Formats

- **Easter revision** — the classic window before May/June exams. [Easter courses →](/tutors/revision-courses/easter/)
- **Half-term intensives** — October and February windows for mid-course correction.
- **Pre-mock revision** — before the January mocks that set predicted grades.
- **Online and residential** — remote delivery for international pupils; Oxford-based options where attendance is possible.

## What a course actually does

A good revision course is not more school. It is: a diagnostic first, then work on the specific papers and mark schemes the pupil will sit, with a tutor who knows the exam board. Group courses suit consolidation; individual formats suit targeted gaps — we advise honestly which is right.

## What to look for — and what we provide

- **Exam-board specificity** — "GCSE maths" is not one exam; courses must match AQA/Edexcel/OCR.
- **Timed practice with marked feedback** — not just coverage.
- **Small groups or individual** — large lecture formats are poor value at this stage.
- **Pricing transparency** — ⏳ course fees confirmed per programme; tutoring-equivalent rates from £65/hour apply for individual formats.

## Questions

**Revision course or regular tutoring?** Complementary, not interchangeable: tutoring builds the base; revision converts it under exam conditions.
**How far ahead to book?** Easter courses fill by January–February; earlier is safer.
