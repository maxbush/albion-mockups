# A-Level Tutors

A-Levels are the grades that decide university offers: a conditional offer reads "A*AA" — three letters that open or close a corridor. Preparation here is not "improve the subject" but "hit the grade boundary the offer requires".

## Subject choice is half the strategy

A-Level choices are made in Year 11 and lock the university map:

- **medicine** — chemistry is non-negotiable, plus biology;
- **engineering, economics at top departments** — maths and often further maths;
- **humanities** — "facilitating subjects" keep options open.

A wrong combination can quietly close Oxbridge courses two years before the application. We check subject sets against the actual university route — before the choices are filed.

## How tutoring works

- **Subject specialists per A-Level** — the exam boards (AQA, Edexcel, OCR, CIE) differ; we match to the board.
- **Mark-scheme literacy** — top grades come from knowing what examiners reward, not only knowing the subject.
- **Predicted grades** — schools predict from Year 12 performance; early work pays twice, in the prediction and the final.
- **Retake strategy** — where a first attempt underdelivered, retakes are planned carefully: some universities view them coldly, others neutrally. The answer is course-specific.
