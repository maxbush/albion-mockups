# GCSE Tutors

GCSE results do two jobs at once: they set the corridor for sixth-form entry and they stay on the university file — Oxford admissions tutors still read them. Weak GCSEs are hard to repair later; that is why this stage deserves the same seriousness as A-Level.

## What GCSE tutoring involves

- **8–10 subjects**, with English, maths and sciences compulsory; options chosen with the sixth-form route in mind.
- **Exam boards matter** — AQA, Edexcel, OCR differ in paper style; preparation targets the specific board.
- **For international pupils** the gap is usually not the subject but academic English and exam technique — the examiners' mark schemes reward a specific kind of answer.

## How we work

1. **Diagnostics** on past papers — where the child stands against the grade scale, not school impressions.
2. **A plan by subject** — what to strengthen, in what order, to the exam date.
3. **Tutor matching** — subject specialists, UK-qualified where board-specificity matters.
4. **Mock cycles** — full timed runs before the real series.

## Rates

From £65/hour; the number of hours follows diagnostics, not a package.

## Questions

**Can GCSEs be prepared for from abroad?** Yes — most of our pupils sit them while at boarding school or before arrival; tutoring runs online.
**One tutor or several?** Usually several — subject specificity matters at GCSE. The coordinator runs them as one plan.
**Is it too late in Year 11?** Not necessarily — a term of focused work often moves a grade boundary. Diagnostics answers honestly.
