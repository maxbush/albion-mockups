# IB Tutors

The IB Diploma is a different machine: six subjects, the Extended Essay, Theory of Knowledge, CAS — forty-five points built from coursework and finals across two years. It rewards organisation as much as ability, and it punishes drift early in the course.

## Where IB pupils lose points

- **Internal assessments (IAs)** — coursework in every subject, moderated by the IB; topic choice and structure are half the grade.
- **The Extended Essay** — a 4,000-word research paper; supervisor support inside school varies wildly.
- **ToK** — the strangest component for new families; a distinct skill, teachable like any other.
- **HL/SL balance** — Higher Level choices carry university weight; the split should be argued, not defaulted.

## How we work

- Subject tutors for HL/SL papers.
- IA and EE supervision within IB rules — we guide structure, argument and draft review; the work remains the pupil's.
- Exam technique for the May/November sessions.
- Coordination with the university route: UK universities make offers on total points plus HL grades — we prepare to the offer, not to the abstract "7".

## A note from practice

The IB's integrity rules are strict — externally produced coursework is detectable and disqualifying. Our support is structured exactly at the line: direction, critique, method — never substitution.

## Questions

**IB or A-Level for a UK university?** Both are read properly by admissions; IB suits broad, organised pupils; A-Level suits depth. For specific courses (medicine, engineering) the subject mix matters more than the programme — see [A-Level →](/tutors/a-level/).
**Mid-course support?** Yes — entering in Year 12 of the diploma is common; the plan starts from current standings.
