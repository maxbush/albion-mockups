# Tutors for the British Curriculum: GCSE, A-Level, IB

More than 150 tutors work with ALBION — selected and coordinated by a dedicated specialist, not drawn from a marketplace. Most are UK-qualified teachers and examiners; several teach within the Oxford system.

## What tutoring at ALBION is

- **A team, not a directory.** We match the tutor to the brief — the subject, the exam board, the child's temperament — and the consultant keeps the whole picture: tutoring, admissions and guardianship in one hand.
- **Goal-driven, not open-ended.** Sessions serve a named objective: an entry exam, a target grade, a scholarship paper. Progress is measured against the brief.
- **Aligned with the admissions route.** For pupils heading to British schools and universities, the tutor sees the admissions requirements — subject choices, boards, formats — rather than teaching "in general".
