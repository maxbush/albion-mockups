# Oxford Summer Schools

Oxford is our home ground — we select summer programmes here from inside the city, not from aggregators. For a child, two weeks in Oxford is also a first reading of the place where the British system is at its most concentrated.

## Programmes we work with ⏳

**Day camps with English — ages 6–12.**
Activity-based programmes run in partnership with British camp operators, alongside local children (~85% English-speaking): two English lessons daily, 40+ activities, groups capped at 12 by level. Oxford locations include **Chandlings School** (60 acres, five miles from the city) and **Headington School** (an established independent school minutes from the centre). From £595 per week; weekly blocks through July–August.

**Academic and university-prep programmes — ages 12–18.**
- **Dulwich College** (ages 12–14) — a summer boarding course at one of London's historic independent schools.
- **Earlscliffe School** (ages 13–17) — summer and spring courses in a boarding-school format.
- **Dukes Cambridge** (ages 14–17) — faculties for university preparation: medicine (run with Medic Portal, a medical-admissions specialist), STEM, art and design, English pathway; two-week sessions from late June.
- **InvestIN** (ages 15–18) — career-immersion programmes: medicine, law, engineering, investment banking, with practising professionals.

## What "Oxford" means in this market — honestly

The word "Oxford" sells, and many commercial camps rent a name. The questions that separate substance from scenery: who teaches, where pupils sleep, how many British children are in the room, what the certificate actually certifies. We can answer these for every programme we place — we are located here and see the provision directly.

## Logistics and guardianship

- Transfers from London airports — arranged.
- For unaccompanied minors — the same guardianship contour as our school pupils: a named adult, 24/7 contact, airport handover.
- Parents are welcome to base themselves in Oxford for the duration — day formats make this practical.

## Booking

July–August sessions open early in the year; the strongest programmes close by spring. Fees from £595/week (day) and ⏳ per programme (residential).
