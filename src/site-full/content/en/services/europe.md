# Universities in Europe for International Applicants

Many families' "European dream" is now more practical than the American one: closer, more affordable, and in a number of countries English-language programmes run from the first year. ALBION manages applications to European universities alongside the British route — a combined list often protects the whole strategy.

## Where English-language bachelor's degrees exist

- **The Netherlands** — the strongest offering for internationals in English: research universities and universities of applied sciences, broad choice of programmes.
- **Italy** — English-language programmes at Bocconi, Bologna and a number of universities; the entrance logic differs from the UK.
- **Spain** — private universities with English-language programmes (IE, ESADE), state ones mostly in Spanish.
- **Germany** — a few English-language bachelor's degrees, more at master's; German-language study is nearly free.
- **France** — Sciences Po and grandes écoles with English tracks; strict selection.

## What to know before applying

- **Deadlines differ from UCAS:** some countries admit by direct application, others through their own systems (Studielink, Parcoursup).
- **Documents** need apostilles and certified translations — start earlier.
- **Cost** — from free (German public universities, in German) to £15,000–20,000/year for English-language private programmes.
- **A combined strategy** — UK + Europe in one list reduces risk without doubling the workload.

## How we work

The same mechanics as the British track: assessment → shortlist across countries → documents and deadlines by each country's system → offers and enrolment. For a child at a British school we often build a paired list: UCAS five + two or three European options.
