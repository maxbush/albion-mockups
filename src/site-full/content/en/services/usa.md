# Universities in the USA: Applications and Preparation

The American track is a different system: not UCAS but the Common App; not five choices but a list built on admissions logic; not subject exams but SAT/ACT, essays and the whole-person profile. ALBION manages US applications for pupils on a British track — and for families who choose America from the start.

## What differs from the UK

- **Timeline.** Early Decision/Early Action deadlines fall in November; Regular Decision in January. Preparation begins a year to eighteen months out.
- **SAT/ACT** — standardised tests; since the pandemic many universities are test-optional, but a strong score still helps.
- **Essays** — a personal essay plus supplements for every university: several texts, each with its own purpose.
- **Profile** — activities, leadership and the story of who you are count alongside grades: admission is holistic.
- **Cost** — $60,000–90,000/year at private universities; financial aid for internationals exists at a limited number of institutions.

## Who the US track suits

- families choosing the American system deliberately;
- pupils for whom a US + UK combined list hedges the risk;
- candidates with strong non-academic profiles — US admissions reads them better than the British system.

## How we work

Assessment → country strategy (US only or combined) → the list and deadlines → SAT/ACT preparation with tutors → essays and supplements → the Common App and follow-through. For underage pupils the guardianship and logistics contour is built in.
