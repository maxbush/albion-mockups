# Career Guidance for Teenagers

"Which A-Levels?" is really a career question asked early. Subject choices at 14–16 close and open university courses; career guidance exists so those choices are made with a map, not by elimination.

## What the programme does

- **Profiling** — structured aptitude and interest testing, not a quiz: verbal, numerical and spatial reasoning plus interests mapping.
- **A specialist conversation** — interpreting the profile against real professions: what the work actually is, what the routes in look like, what pays and what it demands.
- **The educational chain backwards** — from profession → degree → university → A-Level/IB subjects → today's decisions. The output is concrete: which subjects to choose and why.
- **A written route map** — kept by the family and updated as the picture clarifies.

## When it is worth it

- **Year 9 / GCSE options** — the first real fork.
- **Year 11 / A-Level choices** — the decisive one for university entry.
- **Pre-university** — when the degree choice is undecided and the stakes are highest.

## From practice

The most common outcome is not a named profession but a corridor: two or three directions the profile supports, with the subject choices that keep all of them open. That is often the better answer — a 15-year-old rarely needs a final verdict, but always needs doors kept open.
