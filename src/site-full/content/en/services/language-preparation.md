# Language Preparation in Oxford and London

ALBION organises English-language study in the language schools of Oxford and London — for children and adults, in groups or individually. The task is usually concrete: bring academic English to the level a British school or university expects, or prepare for a specific exam.

## Who it is for

- **Children before school entry** — academic English, exam preparation (UKiset, IELTS for schools), classroom confidence.
- **Teenagers** — intensive English alongside subject work; exam English for GCSE/A-Level and university entry.
- **Adults** — general and professional English, IELTS/TOEFL preparation for university and executive programmes.
- **Families** — combined programmes where children and parents study in parallel.

## Formats

- **Group courses** in accredited language schools — Oxford and London centres.
- **Individual tuition** — ALBION tutors, online or in person in Oxford.
- **Combined** — language school plus subject tutoring where the goal is school or university entry, not just fluency.

## What we check before placing a student

A language school is not a commodity: schools differ in student mix, teaching quality and the honesty of their levels. We select on the same criteria as summer programmes — who is in the classroom, who teaches, what the course actually delivers — and we are on the ground in Oxford to see it.

## Questions

**Language school or tutor?** Immersion and volume — school; targeted gaps and exam technique — tutor. Most admission-bound students need both, sequenced correctly.
**How long?** An honest answer follows a level test — but think in months, not weeks, for academic English.
