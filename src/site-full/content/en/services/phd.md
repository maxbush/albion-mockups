# PhD Applications at British Universities

A doctorate is not a taught programme: admission is decided by the supervisor and the research proposal, not by an application form. The mechanics differ fundamentally from undergraduate and master's routes — and need to be understood before anything is submitted.

## How doctoral admission works

1. **Research proposal** — a 1,500–3,000-word document that sets out the question, the literature and the method. This is the decisive document.
2. **Finding a supervisor** — direct contact with academics whose work intersects yours; most places are decided at this stage, before any formal application.
3. **Formal application** — to the university once a supervisor has signalled interest.
4. **Interview** — a research conversation with the prospective supervisor and panel.
5. **Funding** — separate competitions: UKRI, university studentships, departmental funds. Deadlines often fall earlier than admission itself.
