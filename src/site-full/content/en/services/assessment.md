# Academic Assessment

Before any shortlist, before a single school visit, we establish where the child actually stands. Assessment is the foundation of the whole admissions plan — and the point where we will sometimes tell a family something they did not expect to hear.

## What the assessment shows

- **Academic level against British peers** — on real past papers, not on school grades from a different system.
- **English and reasoning profile** — the gap that decides readiness for UKiset, CAT4 or school exams.
- **The realistic corridor** — which tier of schools is achievable, and what needs to change to reach higher.
- **A written report** — yours to keep, whatever you decide next.

## How it runs

1. **Document review** — school reports, history, any prior testing.
2. **Diagnostic testing** — past papers and aptitude measures by target entry point.
3. **Sessions with teachers** — subject specialists probe what tests cannot: thinking, confidence, learning style.
4. **Family conversation and written report** — findings, honest verdict, recommended route.

## Why families trust it — even when it is uncomfortable

The alternative is paying for applications to schools that were never going to say yes, or under-aiming out of caution. In our practice the assessment regularly changes the plan in both directions: sometimes we advise more ambitious targets than the family believed possible; sometimes we say plainly that a dream school needs another year of preparation — and that honest answer is the deliverable.
