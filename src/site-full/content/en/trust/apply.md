# Apply

## Initial consultation — £350

One conversation to map the system for your family: where the child stands, which route is realistic, what it costs and by when. You receive a written report — it stays with you whether or not we work together. The fee is credited in full toward a programme.

## The form

⏳ _Fields: family name, email, phone/messenger, child's age and current school, target (school / university / executive / tutoring / summer / guardianship), desired timing, brief context. Privacy notice + consent checkbox._

## What happens next

1. We reply within one working day to schedule the consultation — in Oxford or by video.
2. Before the call we may ask for school reports or prior test results — the consultation is better with them.
3. After the call: the written report, and if the route makes sense — a proposal with scope and fees.
