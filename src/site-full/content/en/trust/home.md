# British Education, Managed End to End

**ALBION is an Oxford-based educational consultancy.** Since 2010 we have guided international families into British schools, universities and executive programmes — from the first assessment to life after enrolment.

We work independently: no commissions from schools or universities, one accountable team for the whole route.

---

## What we do

**[Private schools →](/private-schools/)**
Boarding and day school selection, entrance exams, applications and settling in. 96% of our pupils received a place at a school from their agreed list.

**[University admissions →](/university-admissions/)**
UCAS strategy, Oxbridge tests and interviews, master's degrees. 3 out of 4 of our Oxbridge candidates reach interview.

**[Executive education →](/executive-education/)**
MBA, EMBA and management programmes — led by a Saïd Business School graduate. 10+ clients completed Saïd programmes in the past year.

**[Tutors →](/tutors/)**
150+ specialists for GCSE, A-Level, IB and entrance exams — selected and coordinated, not a marketplace.

**[Summer schools →](/summer-schools/)**
Academic, language and career programmes in Oxford and beyond, ages 6–18.

**[Guardianship →](/private-schools/guardianship/)**
Professional guardianship to the AEGIS standard — by consultants who know the child from admission.
