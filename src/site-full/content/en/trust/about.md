# About ALBION

## What we are

ALBION is an independent educational consultancy based in Oxford, working since 2010. We manage admissions to British private schools, universities and executive programmes — and stay with the child after enrolment: tutoring, guardianship, the life of the pupil at school.

## The principle: independence

We take no commissions from schools or universities. When we say a school fits your child, that is an assessment — not a paid recommendation. The same rule shapes our diagnostics: if the picture is uncomfortable, we say so at assessment, not after a contract.

## One accountable team

Consulting and academic preparation sit in the same company. The tutor sees the admissions goal; the consultant sees the real progress; the guardian knows the child from the first meeting. When strategy lives in one agency and preparation in another, that synchronisation is much harder to achieve.

## In figures

- **since 2010** — continuous practice in British admissions
- **150+ tutors** — selected and coordinated in-house
- **96%** — pupils who received a place at a school from their agreed list
- **3 of 4** — Oxbridge candidates reaching interview
- **10+** — clients completing Saïd Business School programmes in the past year
