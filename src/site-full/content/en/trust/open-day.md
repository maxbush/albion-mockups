# Open Day

Several times a year we open the office at New Barclay House for families who want to see how we work before deciding anything.

## What happens

- **Meet the team** — the consultants who will actually run your case, not a sales representative.
- **A taste of assessment** — a short diagnostic so you see the method, not only hear about it.
- **Honest Q&A** — fees, timelines, realistic outcomes; the questions agencies usually defer.
- **Oxford context** — for families considering schools or summer programmes nearby, the geography makes sense on the ground.
