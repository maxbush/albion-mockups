# The Oxford Executive MBA

The Oxford EMBA is the Saïd MBA for people who cannot leave their jobs: a modular programme of residential weeks in Oxford spread over roughly two years, for senior managers with typically 8–15+ years of experience.

## Who it is for

- Senior managers and directors who stay in role throughout.
- Founders and owners who need the frameworks and the network without a career pause.
- Candidates for whom a full-time year is the wrong instrument — the EMBA is not a "weaker MBA", it is a different one.

## Format and structure

- **~21 months**, modular: residential teaching weeks in Oxford, roughly monthly ⏳ (verify current schedule).
- **Cohort:** more senior than the MBA — average experience 10+ years, average age mid-30s to 40s.
- **Curriculum:** the Saïd core adapted to executives, electives, a group project, global modules.
- **The employer question:** most EMBA candidates are part-sponsored or self-funded; the employer conversation is part of the application logic and we help structure it.

## Admissions — different mechanics from the MBA

- **No universal GMAT requirement** ⏳ — Saïd assesses executive experience directly; a test may be requested for some profiles.
- **Essays and references** — the leadership narrative and organisational sponsorship matter more than test scores.
- **Interview** — a senior-level conversation about trajectory and purpose.
- **Rolling/round deadlines** ⏳ — verify current dates.

## Cost

- **Tuition:** ⏳ ~£120,000 (verify). Employer sponsorship and payment plans are common.

## Questions

**EMBA or MBA?** If you can pause your career and want acceleration or a pivot — MBA. If you stay in role and need the programme to travel around your work — EMBA. If uncertain, a consultation resolves it faster than research does.
**Is the degree identical?** Yes — the same University of Oxford MBA credential, delivered in executive format.
**Can a company sponsor?** Yes — it is the norm. We help frame the sponsorship case.
