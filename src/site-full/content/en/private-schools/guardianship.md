# Guardianship for International Pupils in the UK

Every British school requires an international pupil under 18 to have an official guardian in the country — the person answerable for the child whenever the school is closed, and for everything the school cannot resolve. The requirement is formal. The responsibility is not.

A guardian "for the paperwork" is easy to find. The question to ask before signing is different: what exactly will this person do when something goes wrong — on a Sunday evening, on an exeat weekend, in a hospital, in a conversation with a school that has made a mistake.

## What the school and the law require

- **School:** an official UK guardian for every international pupil under 18 — a condition of enrolment.
- **Child Student visa:** the guardian is named in the documents; the details are checked.
- **Exeats and half-terms:** several times a term the school closes entirely — the child must be collected, housed and returned.
- **Emergencies:** illness, injury, a disciplinary matter — the school needs someone who answers and arrives.

## Three models of guardianship — honestly

**A nominal guardian** — "a name for the visa". Cheap, but the function ends at the signature. When a real situation arises, the family is alone — thousands of miles away and in another language.

**A host family** — a practical arrangement for weekends. Useful as a supplement, but a host family does not negotiate with the school, does not manage medical care and is not accountable for the academic picture.

**Professional guardianship** — a responsible adult with authority: liaison with school and family, exeat logistics, medical care, the pastoral layer, crisis response. We work to the AEGIS standard — the British benchmark for guardianship organisations.

## Questions

**Is a guardian needed if the child is 16 or 17?**
Yes — under 18 it is a firm requirement of schools and of visa logic, regardless of how independent the pupil is.

**Can a relative in London act as guardian?**
Formally a school may accept a close relative. But test the reality: can they drive to a school in another county on any evening, negotiate in English and take decisions about the child's health?

**What does ALBION guardianship include?**
Continuous contact with school and family, exeat arrangements, 24/7 emergency cover, medical and pastoral support, and tutor coordination where needed.

**Is guardianship only for boarding pupils?**
The main demand is boarding, but a guardian is also needed by international day pupils and under-age college students.
