# Resources

This section holds three guides we wrote for families who are working out how British admissions fit together. Each is a full article, read on the page, and each links on to the pages where the detail sits.

## The guides

**[Parents' Guide to British Schools](/resources/parents-guide/).** Prep and senior schools, boarding and day, the entry points at 7+, 11+, 13+ and 16+, the route an application takes, and the decisions that rest with the family and not the school. Start here if the system is new to you.

**[Entrance tests guide](/resources/entrance-tests-guide/).** What the ISEB Pre-Test, CAT4, UKiset and Common Entrance are for, which university tests belong to which course, and how to prepare without overdoing it. Read it once you know which schools or courses you are considering.

**[Admissions interview guide](/resources/interview-guide/).** What school interviewers listen for, how the Oxford and Cambridge interview differs, and how to talk aloud about what you have written in an application.

## A note on dates

The guides describe the system as it stood when we last checked them. Exam rules, test names, registration windows and UCAS procedure change from year to year, and a school can alter its requirements between one cycle and the next. Use the guides to understand the shape of the process, and confirm every date and requirement with the school, the university or the test provider before you act on it.

## If you would like to talk it through

A guide cannot tell you which of these routes suits your child. If you would like a person to look at the age, the current school and the timetable with you, tell us about the situation in the form, and we will invite you to an initial consultation.
