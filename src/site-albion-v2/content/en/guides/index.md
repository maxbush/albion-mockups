# PDF guides from ALBION

There is nothing to download yet: the guides are still being written. Leave an email address and a link to each one will follow when it is published.

## Three subjects in preparation

- **UK schools.** For parents at the start of choosing a school. Until the guide is out, our page on [private schools in the UK](/private-schools/) gives the wider picture.
- **School entrance exams.** On the tests used for entry to UK schools, UKiset among them. The age-by-age basics are already on the [11+, 13+ and 16+ entrance exams](/private-schools/entrance-exams/) page.
- **Oxbridge.** On preparing an application to Oxford or Cambridge. The process itself is described on our [Oxbridge admissions](/university-admissions/oxbridge/) page.

Length, structure and release dates are not fixed for any of them, and we will not announce them before the material is ready.
