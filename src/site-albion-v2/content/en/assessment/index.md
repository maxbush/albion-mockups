# Academic assessment: an honest picture of where your child stands

Parents of a child educated outside the UK often know how the child is doing at home, but not how that compares with pupils at British independent schools. ALBION's academic assessment settles that question before schools are chosen and before preparation time is spent. It is run online by ALBION teachers and suits children from overseas schools, including international schools that follow the British curriculum. The scheme is built for sixth form entry.

## What you receive

For each subject the teacher writes a detailed report. Consultants add an overall profile, recommendations and a view on whether the chosen A-Level combination makes sense. Your child is set against British peers from independent schools, the gaps are named, and the outlook for admission is realistic rather than reassuring.

A disappointing result is still a useful one. If the schools you have in mind look out of reach for now, we say so and suggest another timeline, another entry point or a different list.

## How the assessment runs

English is checked before anything else. A child who cannot yet follow the tasks and the teacher easily will score below their real ability, and the assessment would end up measuring language. Where it is needed, English lessons come first.

Subjects follow. The format depends on the child's starting point and can include an online test, a written task, a written paper and a lesson with a teacher. Up to four of the A-Level subjects the student intends to study are assessed. Each has a one-to-one online lesson, and the student also sits past papers under exam conditions.

Teachers look at depth of understanding, analysis and interpretation, written work, critical thinking and readiness for sixth form, and form a view of the student's potential in each subject at A-Level.

## What the reports are used for

The reports feed straight into planning. Consultants draw up a first list of up to ten schools, explain the application process and each school's entry requirements, then help you settle on five to apply to. The same reports set the lesson plan: which gaps to close first, and how intensively. Whoever reads the findings also prepares the child for [entrance exams](/private-schools/entrance-exams/), so nothing has to be explained twice.

Where gaps appear, our [A-Level tutors](/tutors/a-level/) take the work forward in the subjects chosen. For the wider route to a place, see the guide to [private schools](/private-schools/); [boarding schools](/private-schools/boarding/) have their own page.

## Questions and answers

### How much does the assessment cost?

The price depends on the scope of the work, and we give it after you complete the application form.

### Should my child prepare in advance?

Usually not. The point is to see the current level as it is, which is why past papers and a lesson with a teacher are used.

### Is this only for sixth form entry?

The scheme described here, built on A-Level subjects, is designed for sixth form entry. What can be offered for earlier entry points is discussed separately, after the form.

### Does the assessment replace entrance exams?

No. It is diagnostic and gives no access to a school. It informs you and your consultants, while each school still judges the child with its own tests and interview.
