# Our team

Five people run ALBION's work, and each is profiled below with role and qualifications, so you know who may be advising you before the first call. The tutors who teach alongside them are described at the end.

## Anfisa Bashkirova, MSc Oxon

Co-founder. Anfisa holds an MSc in Major Programme Management from Saïd Business School, University of Oxford. Before moving into education consultancy she managed risk internationally at Intel, and she still reads a situation by asking what could go wrong before settling on the obvious route. She has worked in British education for 16 years. As an alumni representative for Saïd, she takes part in the recruitment of candidates and has seen that process from the school's side.

## Altynay Yeshmatova, LLM

Co-founder and Head of Guardianship. Altynay specialises in admission to independent schools and has worked in school consultancy since 2010. She holds a Master of Research in law from the University of Buckingham. She knows the system from two sides: as an adviser, and as a parent whose own children went through boarding schools in England.

## William Knox, MA, MLitt, MSc

Academic Director. William advises on admission to universities in the UK, the USA and Europe, and on master's and executive programmes. He holds an MLitt in Creative Writing from the University of Glasgow and an MA (Hons) in English from the University of Aberdeen. His academic mentoring is combined with management experience in education, and the depth of his knowledge is apparent within minutes of talking to him.

## Vladimir Ivanov-Krymskiy, MSc

Director of Development. Vladimir is a graduate of a British business school with a master's degree, and he built a language school of his own, so he knows education from the founder's side as well as the customer's. At ALBION he is responsible for strategy and the partner network.

## Jonathan Mintram, BA (Hons), CELTA

Jonathan selects every ALBION tutor personally and builds the teaching team for one-to-one lessons and academic courses. A tutor is chosen for a specific pupil's needs, not pulled from a general list.

The pool numbers 150+ across time zones, and together they deliver 3,000+ hours of teaching a year. Many have long academic and practical careers, some with decades in the classroom. They prepare pupils for GCSE, A-Level and IB and for school and university entrance tests, and they teach at home or on location for families who travel. How tutors and consultants work together is covered in [about ALBION](/about/), and levels and formats are under [tutors](/tutors/).
