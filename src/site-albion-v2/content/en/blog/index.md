# The ALBION blog

ALBION advisers and tutors write the blog for parents and applicants who are choosing a school, preparing for exams or planning a university application. No prior knowledge of the British system is assumed, and terms are explained where they appear.

## Articles

- [Oxford acceptance rate](/blog/oxford-acceptance-rate/): the 2024 admissions statistics, offer and admission rates, and the spread by course and country.
- [A-Level or IB](/blog/a-level-or-ib/): how the qualifications are built, what universities ask for and how to choose for a course.
- [The 11+ exam](/blog/11-plus-exam/): how grammar schools and independent schools differ, which tests exist and how to prepare.
- [The cost of British education](/blog/cost-of-british-education/): school, university and living costs in figures.

## Guides

- [Parents' guide](/resources/parents-guide/): how British schools are organised, stages and entry points.
- [Guide to entrance tests](/resources/entrance-tests-guide/): ISEB, UKiset, CAT4 and tests for universities.
- [Guide to the admissions interview](/resources/interview-guide/): the interview for a school and for Oxford or Cambridge.

## How the pieces are written

Each piece is written as a guide: a short answer to the question, then the detail and the limits, then what it means for a particular family, then a sensible next step. Figures that change from year to year are tied to the year and source they come from. For a question about your own situation, the [application form](/apply/) is the better start.
