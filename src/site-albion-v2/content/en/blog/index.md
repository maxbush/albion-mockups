# The ALBION blog

ALBION advisers and tutors write the blog for parents and applicants who are choosing a school, preparing for exams or planning a university application. No prior knowledge of the British system is assumed, and terms are explained where they appear.

## Subjects

- **Schools:** boarding and day schools, entry points, choosing by age.
- **Exams:** entrance tests for schools and universities, preparation and timing.
- **University admissions:** UCAS, Oxbridge, master's study. Questions such as the Oxford acceptance rate belong here.
- **MBA:** programmes for working professionals, including Saïd Business School at Oxford.

## What an article looks like

Each article is written as a guide: a short answer to the question, then the detail and the limits, then what it means for a particular family, then a sensible next step. Figures that change from year to year are tied to the year and source they come from.

For a question about your own situation, the [application form](/apply/) is the better start. Longer reference material is collected in the [guides](/resources/).
