# English language preparation in the UK

A family asking about language preparation usually has one of two things in mind. One is an exam with a date attached: IELTS, DELF or the Goethe-Zertifikat. The other is immersion, meaning a course at a language school in Oxford, London or elsewhere. ALBION arranges both, and the first conversation settles which of them the goal actually requires.

## One-to-one preparation for an exam

Individual tuition suits a fixed exam and a fixed date.

| Exam | Language | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| IELTS | English | university and programme applications that ask for a certificate |
| DELF | French | French-language programmes, or a recognised record of level |
| Goethe-Zertifikat | German | German-language programmes |

ALBION's tutors also teach English, French, Chinese and other languages outside an exam setting. Lessons can take place online or in person. Tutors are spread across time zones, so the timetable is fitted around the student and not the other way round. Teaching at home or while travelling is covered under [homeschooling](/tutors/homeschooling/).

No score or date can be promised. The result depends on the starting level and on how steadily the student works, so the work begins with a check of level, and we say honestly what is achievable by the exam date.

## Language school places

A language school suits a child who needs English good enough to study in a British school, or an adult who wants an environment and not only lessons. Courses can be in a group or one to one. We match the school to age, level, length of stay and budget, and the recommendation follows the family's aim, not a provider's convenience.

School names and fees vary with age, season and length of course, so we give them after an initial consultation.

Where a child is heading for a British school, English is checked at the very start, since it affects the rest of the plan. If the gap is large, intensive study comes before entrance assessments. The page on [private school admissions](/private-schools/) explains how those fit together.

## Language and university plans

For many applicants a certificate is simply one more deadline. The score required depends on the programme, and the date on the intake. That is why language work is planned together with [university admissions](/university-admissions/): the adviser knows the target score, and the tutor knows the date. Younger students can also look at a [summer school](/summer-schools/), where lessons are combined with leisure.

## Questions and answers

### Does a child need a language school before applying to a British school?

It depends on current English. We check the level first, and if the gap is substantial, intensive study precedes entrance tests instead of running alongside them.

### Group lessons or one to one?

A group gives immersion and conversation. One-to-one lessons are built around the exam and the student's pace. Where there is a specific exam with a date, individual tuition is usually the natural fit.

### Can I prepare for IELTS remotely?

Yes. ALBION's tutors work with students anywhere in the world and across time zones, and the timetable is agreed before lessons begin.

### Do you teach languages other than English?

Yes. Tutors teach French, Chinese and other languages, and prepare students for DELF and the Goethe-Zertifikat as well as IELTS.

### How much does it cost?

There is no fixed price. Language school fees depend on the school, the student's age, the season and the length of stay. Tutoring is charged by the level of the tutor, and the guide rates are on the [prices page](/prices/). We give the total after an initial consultation.
