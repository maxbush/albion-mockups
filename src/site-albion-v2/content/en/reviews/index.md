# Client Reviews

ALBION is rated 5.0 on Google. Clients write and publish those reviews themselves, so our Google profile is the place to read them.

This page carries no pull-quotes, and we will not add some to make it look fuller. What a family says about a child's school, a university application or a budget is personal, and it appears here only when the author has agreed.

## What to look for in any consultancy's reviews

A rating on its own tells you little. It helps to know which service the reviewer used (school admissions, university applications, tutoring), how long ago it was, and whether they describe actual steps rather than a general impression. If you want to see how results and setbacks are reported, the [results page](/about/results/) points to stories that are each told with their limits.

## How families hear about progress

Feedback does not wait for the end of an engagement. Once a child is registered with schools and preparing for entrance exams, consultants report to the parents every quarter on how lessons are going and how ready the child is. After the academic diagnostic for Sixth Form entry, teachers write a detailed report on each subject, and consultants add an overall profile of the student with recommendations.

If something is not working, tell us directly.
