# UCAS Deadline, Application and Offers

The UCAS deadline depends on the course, not on the applicant. Oxford, Cambridge and most medicine, dentistry and veterinary courses close in October; nearly everything else closes in January. For 2027 entry the October date is 15 October 2026 and the January date is 13 January 2027. Not every family needs an adviser, and we say plainly when your own effort is enough.

## UCAS deadlines for 2027 entry

These are the UCAS dates for the 2027 cycle, checked against UCAS on 12 October 2026. Deadlines fall at 18:00 UK time.

| Date | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1 September 2026 | Completed applications can be submitted; a reference must be in place first |
| 15 October 2026 | Equal consideration date: Oxford, Cambridge, and most medicine, dentistry and veterinary courses |
| 13 January 2027 | Equal consideration date for most other undergraduate courses |
| 25 February 2027 | UCAS Extra opens for applicants with five choices and no offers |
| 30 June 2027 | Last date for applications to be sent to universities; later ones go into Clearing |
| 23 September 2027 | Final date for 2027 entry applications |

The October date for 2027 entry is 15 October 2026. If it has passed, or only hours remain, the second question below covers what to do.

"Equal consideration" means that a complete application, reference included, which arrives by the date is read on the same terms as every other one. A choice with an October date can be combined with choices that have a January date, because the later ones may be added afterwards, up to five in total.

A pupil applying through school should work to the school's own date, which is earlier. The school needs time to check qualifications, write the reference and attach predicted grades, and the application cannot go to UCAS without them.

## What is UCAS?

What does UCAS stand for? The Universities and Colleges Admissions Service. It runs the single route for undergraduate applications to UK universities: one application, up to five course choices, one personal statement, one reference. The application lives in the UCAS Hub, and every applicant receives a Personal ID when they start.

Two limits surprise families. An applicant may choose no more than four courses in any one of medicine, dentistry, veterinary medicine or veterinary science. And, with narrow exceptions, only one course at either Oxford or Cambridge. Our [Oxbridge page](/university-admissions/oxbridge/) explains what else is different on that track, and the wider picture is on the [university admissions page](/university-admissions/).

## What goes into the application

### The personal statement: three UCAS questions

The statement is no longer one long essay. Applicants answer three UCAS questions: why they want this course or subject, how their qualifications and studies have prepared them, and what else they have done outside education and why it is relevant. All three share one limit of 4,000 characters including spaces, with at least 350 characters in each answer.

Every choice receives the same text, so five related courses are far easier to write for than five unrelated ones. UCAS publishes its own personal statement guide, and ours is on the [personal statement page](/university-admissions/personal-statement/).

### The UCAS reference and predicted grades

One referee writes the reference. For a school pupil it is a teacher or tutor, working through the school, who can also enter predicted grades. An applicant without a school asks someone who knows them academically or professionally, and the referee must not be family, a friend or a partner: UCAS may cancel an application that breaks the rule. The application cannot be submitted until the reference is added, which makes the referee the usual bottleneck in the last fortnight.

### Choices, education and payment

Choices are picked from the course search, qualifications are entered with their awarding body and dates, and the fee is paid when the application is sent, either directly or as the school instructs.

## How to accept an offer on UCAS

Replies open once every university has given a decision. UCAS then emails the applicant, who signs in and sees a personal reply date. A firm choice is the first-choice offer. If it is conditional, an insurance choice can be added as a back-up, and UCAS advises picking one with lower conditions that the applicant would still be glad to attend. The firm and insurance cannot be swapped on results day, and all other offers must be declined.

For 2027 entry, the reply date is 5 May 2027 if the last decision arrives by 31 March, 2 June if it arrives by 12 May, and 21 July if it arrives by 14 July. An applicant with no offer they want can use Extra or, later, Clearing.

## Who needs help and who can manage alone

Many families complete UCAS without outside help. A single mainstream course, a school with experience of the system and a referee who knows the pupil is a workable setup.

Help earns its fee elsewhere. It matters when the courses must share one statement, in competitive subjects, and on the Oxbridge and medical tracks with their October date. It matters too for families abroad and for applicants without a school that handles UCAS. A consultant reads the application as an admissions tutor would, and finds weak points while there is time to fix them.

## Questions and answers

### When is the UCAS deadline for 2027 entry?

For Oxford, Cambridge and most medical courses it is 15 October 2026; for most other courses, 13 January 2027. The course listing in UCAS search shows which applies. Courses with other start dates may close earlier, so check the listing before assuming.

### What happens if I miss the UCAS deadline?

If the October date is still ahead, the application can be submitted until 18:00 UK time on the day, provided the reference has been added. After a deadline, for many courses you can still apply, up to the final date in September. Ask the university whether it has places first, especially for October courses, which rarely consider late applications. Many universities accept applications from international students for longer.

### How much are UCAS applications, and what does the fee cover?

The UCAS application fee for the 2027 cycle is £34.50, one payment covering up to five choices. Students who have received free school meals during secondary education, and care leavers, may qualify for a waiver.

### Is there a UCAS reference example I can copy?

Not usefully. The reference is written by the referee, in English, in the application itself, and it covers academic suitability for the chosen courses, predicted grades and, with the applicant's permission, circumstances that affected their work. Read any sample as an illustration of structure and treat nothing as a template.

### Where do I find my UCAS ID?

Also called the Personal ID, it is a ten-digit number shown in the format 123-456-7890 on every email UCAS sends. It does not exist until an application is started, and it is the number UCAS asks for if you contact them.

### What if I change my mind after replying to offers?

Within 14 days of replying, contact UCAS advisers to change a reply. After that it depends on the universities agreeing, and no swaps are possible after 22 July 2027.
