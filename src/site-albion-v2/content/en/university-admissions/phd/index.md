# PhD admissions support

A doctorate means several years of supervision and funding, and the people reading your application are deciding whether to commit them to you. ALBION advises candidates applying for PhD and MPhil/PhD places in the UK and the US, in any discipline from politics to quantum physics. The work suits master's graduates, professionals with postgraduate qualifications, people changing field and those moving from a taught master's onto a research track.

## What the committee is really asking

Admissions decisions on PhD programmes rest on three factors: risk, fit with the department's research, and the potential for funding. A committee turns these into three questions. How coherent is this candidate's research path so far? Can a supervisor realistically be found for the proposed topic? Are years of financial commitment to this person justified?

A good idea fails if nobody in the department can supervise it. A suitable supervisor will not rescue an application that offers no thread from past study to future research. Everything below is arranged to answer all three questions before anything is submitted.

## How an application is prepared

The centre of the work is matching. ALBION identifies programmes and, within them, supervisors whose interests align with your topic. A fit between topic, university and named supervisor decides more outcomes than the ranking of the institution.

Around it sits a roadmap built on your goals, academic profile and target programmes, with one-to-one consultations available on request at each stage. The written file follows: a personal statement strategy, a choice of referees and a check that their letters are consistent, and an academic CV reshaped for doctoral committees. Before submission the whole application gets a final quality check.

Mock interviews follow the format that supervisors use when they meet candidates. If offers arrive, we set them and their funding packages side by side, so the choice rests on comparison and not on whichever reply came first.

Academic director William Knox oversees admissions to universities and master's programmes at ALBION. You can read more about him and the rest of the [team](/team/).

## Where our part ends

No adviser can promise a place or funding. The decision belongs to the university and the supervisor, not to us. What we can do is make the application coherent, put forward realistic pairings of topic and supervisor, and show you early where the weak point is. Fees depend on how many programmes are involved and how much support is wanted; we give the figure after an initial consultation.

If the case for a doctorate is not yet settled, it may be worth starting with [master's admissions](/university-admissions/masters/), which for candidates without research experience is sometimes a sensible intermediate step. ## Questions and answers

### Can I apply for a PhD without a master's degree?

The rules are set by each university and programme. We check them before shortlisting and, where the base qualification falls short, suggest an intermediate step, for example a master's programme.

### Will you find my supervisor?

Supervisor matching is part of the service: we compare their research interests with your topic and propose a few realistic pairings. The supervisor decides whether to take a student, and the strength of the application and interview is what can influence that decision.

### Do I need a settled topic before we start?

You need at least a direction, because programmes and supervisors are matched to it. The wording of the topic can be refined during the one-to-one consultations.

### Can I prepare while working?

Yes. One-to-one sessions are arranged on request and at times that suit you. University deadlines do not move, so it pays to begin early.
