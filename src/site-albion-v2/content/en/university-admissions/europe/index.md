# European university admissions

Two applications in continental Europe rarely look alike. There is no shared portal and no common calendar, and the rules vary by country, by institution and often by programme. ALBION advises families and adult candidates on admissions from the Sorbonne to Bocconi and INSEAD, at undergraduate, master's and doctoral level and in business education.

## One continent, many systems

Deadlines, document lists, requirements for school qualifications and the language of instruction are all set locally. A file that works for one university may need rewriting for the next, and a business-school application is judged on different grounds from a bachelor's. The two are never prepared in the same way.

We therefore start with the candidate, not the map: subjects, exam results, languages and the career the degree is meant to serve. A university is a tool for that career, and each programme is weighed against where the student wants to be in ten years, not only at graduation.

## What the work covers

An adviser sets the plan: which countries, which universities and programmes, and in what order the applications are prepared. ALBION then coordinates the written side of each file, including personal essays, motivation letters, academic references and a portfolio where the course asks for one. Tutors from the same company prepare the candidate for entrance tests, programme exams and language certificates (IELTS, DELF, Goethe-Zertifikat).

Because adviser and tutors belong to one team, a change of plan reaches the lessons at once. William Knox, ALBION's academic director, oversees admissions to universities in Europe, the US and the UK.

## What cannot be promised

Nobody can promise a place, and we do not. ALBION receives no commission from any university, so the shortlist follows the candidate's profile and nothing else. If the language of a chosen programme is not yet within reach, language study comes first and sets the timetable. Fees depend on the country, the number of programmes and the amount of preparation; we quote them after an initial consultation, and the [prices page](/prices/) lists what we publish for other services.

A European track can run beside a British one. The [wider university admissions service](/university-admissions/) keeps both calendars in a single plan.

## Questions and answers

### Can I apply to Europe and the UK in the same cycle?

Yes. Dates and paperwork differ, so the plan is built around one calendar. Some material, such as the academic CV and early essay drafts, carries over. The final text for each programme is rewritten.

### Do I need to speak the national language?

It depends on how the programme is taught. Some courses run in English and others in the local language, in which case a recognised certificate is needed. We prepare candidates for DELF and Goethe-Zertifikat through our [language preparation](/language-preparation/).

### Do you handle master's and doctoral applications in Europe?

Yes, alongside undergraduate and business education. The logic of a master's file is set out on the [master's admissions page](/university-admissions/masters/), and research degrees have their own page on [PhD admissions](/university-admissions/phd/).

### How do European universities treat A-Levels and the IB?

There is no single answer: it depends on the country, the university and the programme. We read the requirements on the university's own site before the student settles on subjects and target grades.
