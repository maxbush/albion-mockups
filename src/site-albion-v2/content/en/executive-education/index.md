# Executive Education: MBA, EMBA and Management Programmes

ALBION is based in Oxford, the city of Saïd Business School, and prepares applications by senior professionals and business owners to programmes in the UK, the US and Europe. At this level the choice shapes your role and your professional circle for years, so the work starts with your profile and your plans. The list of schools comes later.

[Tell us about your career in the form](/apply/)

## Who applies

Most clients are managers and specialists who want a stronger position, a wider remit or a route to the next career stage. Business owners form a distinct group. Some have been away from academic life for years, others are scaling, restructuring or entering new markets.

Some applicants return after a long career break and would rather build their education in stages. Others have moved country for work or family reasons and found that universities and business schools are suddenly within reach. For anyone in or near Oxford the point is practical, since Saïd is in the same city as ALBION.

## Formats we prepare applications for

| Format | Usually suits | What we weigh |
|---|---|---|
| Executive MBA | A manager who keeps working throughout | Seniority of the cohort, module pattern, scale of your responsibility |
| Full-time MBA | A change of function, industry or country, with a career break | Academic model, alumni network, logic of the move |
| Accelerated MBA | A candidate with substantial experience who cannot afford a long pause | Fit between your record and the cohort, intensity of the course |
| Management master's | A specialist moving into management or changing field | Academic expectations, structure of the personal statement |
| Postgraduate diploma | A practitioner who wants applied content for current work | Course content, level of the group, relevance to your business |
| Short executive and leadership programmes | A dense learning environment for a limited time | Who else is in the room, how discussion is run, fit with your task |

A short course does not mean short preparation. The cohort and the style of discussion matter as much in one week as over a longer programme, so the analysis behind the choice is of similar size. The page on [management programmes](/executive-education/management-programmes/) sets out the four degree and diploma groups and the short formats.

## What happens during an application

Work begins with your profile: management level, scale of responsibility, the direction your career has taken and where you want it to go. That analysis produces the list of programmes and one line of argument for each, consistent across all of them. The committee then reads a single coherent set of documents, not a pile of unrelated texts.

We draft the CV, motivation statements, essays and references, and you approve every document and every decision before anything is sent. Deadlines, submission and interview rehearsal stay with us. Analysis, strategy and drafting run as one process, from the first review to the final choice.

The method behind the profile review comes from Anfisa Bashkirova, a co-founder of ALBION. She is a Saïd graduate and works with the school as an alumni representative in candidate recruitment. The method sorts your managerial experience into strengths and gaps and sets it against the academic model of a particular programme. How that looks for degree courses is described under [MBA admissions](/executive-education/mba/).

## Who works on your application

A small team works to one strategy. A lead consultant is responsible for positioning and the overall logic of the application. Mentors with experience in the sector you plan to move into, or stay in, join the work, and where possible an introduction to graduates with a similar profile or goal can be arranged.

Writers and editors with an academic and business background draft and proofread the texts. If a programme asks for the GMAT or GRE, university-level English and mathematics teachers, including native speakers, prepare you for it. The wider [ALBION team](/team/) has its own page.

## What counts as a programme

For us a programme is a separate application, written with materials and a positioning of its own. Two courses that use different selection logic or ask for a different set of essays count as two programmes.

## Independent advice and its limits

ALBION does not represent schools or accept commission from universities, which keeps the shortlist tied to your goals. No outcome can be promised. Admissions committees decide, and competition at strong business schools is consistently high. What we can do is aim the application at the right programme and make sure it tells your story well.

## When a profile holds several careers

Many senior candidates arrive with several lines at once: a business of their own, roles in other companies, an earlier academic or industry career. Laid side by side, they read as separate stories, and a committee struggles to see who is applying. The consultant's job is to choose one line to carry the account and to give the others a supporting place. That work is done before any text is written. Other applicants' stories are on the [cases page](/cases/).

## Questions and answers

### Do I need a GMAT?

On some programmes the GMAT or GRE is part of selection, on others it can be replaced or is not asked for at all. We confirm the rules for each programme at the outset, so that any preparation is planned well before the deadline.

### How much does a programme cost, and what does ALBION charge?

Each school sets its fees and revises them from intake to intake, so check the school's own pages for the current figure. ALBION's fee depends on the number of programmes and the volume of preparation, and is quoted after the form and a first conversation.

### Can I apply to five or six programmes at once?

Not within one engagement: the limit is three. Three applications can each be convincing without contradicting the others. Beyond that the positioning thins, and your own time for reviewing drafts runs out. If you have more candidates, we choose the strongest three during the profile review.

### I have not studied for years and have never written an academic essay. Is that a problem?

It is a familiar starting point. The motivation essay is understood differently in Britain than in many other systems, and adult applicants often write about their company when the committee wants to read about them. Our writers help to find the material, and the final text stays yours.

### Is an English certificate always required?

Requirements vary by programme. Lacking a certificate does not always rule you out: where a programme allows it, a reasoned request to waive the language requirement can be put forward. The school decides, and no outcome can be assured.

### Can I study and keep my executive role?

Yes, with an Executive MBA or certain short programmes. A full-time MBA means leaving work for its duration. Which one fits depends on your role and your aim.
