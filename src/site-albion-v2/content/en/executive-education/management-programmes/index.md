# Executive and Management Programmes

Management programmes differ in length, academic standing and the way applicants are selected, which is why each application is written on its own terms. ALBION works across four groups of programme and the short formats, and matches them to your management role and the next stage of your career.

## What we prepare

Once your profile has been reviewed, ALBION prepares the CV in the form each school expects, along with motivation statements and essays. References are part of the work: the choice of referees and letters written in an academic register. Where a programme requires the GMAT or GRE, university-level mathematics and English teachers prepare you for it. Interview rehearsal and control of submission dates complete the list.

The people behind this are introduced on the [team page](/team/), and figures for adult clients are on the [results page](/about/results/).

## The four groups and the short formats

**Masters.** Management master's degrees for specialists moving into management or changing field. Committees look at academic expectations and at how the personal statement is built. For the general picture, see [master's admissions](/university-admissions/masters/).

**MBA.** Full-time or accelerated. The choice rests on whether you can take a career break and how much experience you already have. The formats are compared on our page about [MBA admissions](/executive-education/mba/).

**EMBA.** A programme for the working manager, taught to a cohort of people with management experience. For Oxford it has a page of its own: [Oxford Executive MBA](/executive-education/mba/oxford-executive-mba/).

**Executive Diploma.** A programme with an applied slant. Among our candidates it was chosen where the horizon was a single year and the subject specific, such as financial strategy or global business. It is prepared as a separate application with its own requirements.

**Short executive and leadership programmes.** These last weeks, yet the cohort and the way discussion is run matter just as much. The analysis before applying is hardly smaller.

## What counts as one programme

One programme means one application that needs its own documents and its own argument. The Executive MBA and the Saïd MBA, for instance, are two, even where the school is the same. Three is the ceiling for one engagement: past that point the applications begin to contradict each other, and the time you have for approving documents runs out before the submission window closes. A long list is therefore cut down at the very start.

## Questions and answers

### How is an Executive Diploma different from an MBA?

An Executive Diploma has an applied slant and a specific subject, where an MBA covers management more broadly. Selection rules differ between programmes, so the applications are written independently. The profile review shows which one fits you.

### Can a management master's work if I am not yet a manager?

That is what the group is for: it is aimed at specialists moving into management or changing field. Selection here rests on the academic expectations of the programme and on the structure of the personal statement, and the preparation is built around those two.

### Is a GMAT needed for every programme?

Not everywhere. The programme sets the rule: some require the test, others replace it or do not ask for it. We establish which before any drafting begins.

### Are short programmes worth it if I cannot spare time for a long course?

When the format and the cohort suit your task, they can be. Choosing one takes the same care as choosing an MBA, so it is compared with the alternatives during the profile review.

### Who writes the essays?

ALBION's writers and editors prepare the texts in constant dialogue with you. The material, the biographical detail and the voice are yours, and you approve the final version.
