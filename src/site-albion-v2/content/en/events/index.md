# Events and webinars

None of ALBION's planned events has a confirmed date yet, and we would rather say so than publish a placeholder. This page lists what is being planned and how to hear about it once a date is fixed.

## What is planned

**Webinars.** Online sessions with ALBION advisers on admission to UK schools and universities. Topics, dates and booking arrangements have not been announced.

**An ALBION open day in Oxford.** An in-person event in the city where our office is based. The format and date are still to be agreed. Everything known so far is on the [open day page](/events/open-day/), which also explains how the event differs from our service for Oxford and Cambridge college open days.

## Hearing first

The interest form asks for your name, your email address and the format you prefer: a webinar, an in-person event or both. When something is confirmed, you receive one email with the date, topic and registration link.

Until then, the [guides](/resources/) and the [blog](/blog/) are the place for questions on schools, exams and applications. If a decision cannot wait for a webinar, a consultant can look at your situation now. Families working towards [Oxford and Cambridge](/university-admissions/oxbridge/) run to a fixed calendar, so an early conversation helps.
