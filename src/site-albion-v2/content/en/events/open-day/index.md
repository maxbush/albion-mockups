# Open days: Oxbridge visits and the ALBION event

Two different things go by this name, and they are easy to confuse. One is a paid service for families applying to Oxford or Cambridge. The other is an ALBION event in Oxford, which has no date yet.

## The Oxbridge open day service

Oxford alone has 32 undergraduate colleges. Each has its own admissions committee, its own open days and its own timetable, and academic departments run subject events on separate calendars. Cambridge works on the same collegiate principle. No family can see everything, so the real question is which colleges and which subjects to visit first.

At an open day a student can judge the academic environment, speak to admissions staff and watch demonstration interviews. ALBION plans the visits so that each one feeds into the wider application strategy, instead of being a day out beside it.

| Part of the service | Fee |
|---|---|
| Strategy and planning for a visit | £350 per visit |
| Accompanied attendance at the open day | £350 per visit |

Both parts come at no extra charge with the [full Oxbridge admissions programme](/university-admissions/oxbridge/). Fees for the programme itself are on the [prices page](/prices/).

## The ALBION Open Day in Oxford

This is a separate event that ALBION plans to hold in Oxford for parents and applicants. The date, programme and format have not been decided, so no booking is open for a particular day.

To receive an invitation, leave your name, your email address and whether a parent, an applicant or both would attend. One email will follow once the date is confirmed. Other plans are listed under [events](/events/).

If your Oxbridge timetable is already tight, do not wait for the event. You can reach the team through the [contact page](/contact/), or describe your situation to an adviser directly.
