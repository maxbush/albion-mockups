University Admissions in the UK

Master's Degrees in the UK for International Applicants

A master's is the most frequent request we receive — and the freest application of all: there is no unified UCAS here, every university and programme sets its own deadlines, requirements and package of documents.

Book a consultation

What determines admission

  • Degree and transcript. The British system reads both the university and the classification; conversion tables between systems are a nuance we handle.
  • Statement / essays. For a master's these are programme-specific and argue the career logic — why this programme, after this experience.
  • References. Academic or professional — selected by programme requirements.
  • English. IELTS/TOEFL with programme thresholds; at strong universities the requirement is 7.0–7.5 overall.
  • Interview. For competitive programmes — an academic or professional conversation.

Deadlines — programmes differ

There is no common date: some programmes admit on a rolling basis, others by rounds. The most competitive close months ahead — a missed window means waiting a year. We build the calendar for each chosen programme.

Funding

More options than at undergraduate level: Chevening, university scholarships, departmental funding for strong profiles. We calculate which schemes your profile qualifies for at consultation — and say plainly where there is nothing to count on.

The adult track

For candidates with career experience there is a separate line: MBAs, executive programmes, management degrees at business schools. Requirements and logic differ — see Executive Education →.

Costs

  • Tuition: typically £25,000–45,000 for the year, depending on programme.
  • ALBION: consultation with written report — £350; admissions programme — individual, by package of documents.

Questions

Does a bachelor's from a lesser-known home university count?+

In most cases — yes; it is read through the lens of grading, transcript and programme relevance.

An English-language degree, no IELTS?+

Some universities waive the test if prior education was in English — decided per programme.

Realistic timeframe?+

Comfortably — 6–12 months before programme start, including the language test.
