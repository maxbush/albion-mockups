MBA in the UK: How to Choose and How to Get In

The Oxford Saïd MBA

The Saïd Business School MBA is a one-year programme inside the University of Oxford: business school rigour plus the collegiate university — matriculation, a college community, the Oxford network. For many of our clients it is the strongest single year an international career can buy.

Book a consultation

What the programme looks like

  • Duration: 12 months, September to August.
  • Cohort: ~300 students, overwhelmingly international; average experience ~5–6 years, average age ~29.
  • Structure: core courses, electives, the entrepreneurship project, a summer elective or internship window.
  • Character: Saïd is strongest where business meets social impact and entrepreneurship — its positioning is distinctive and should shape the essays.

Admissions

  • GMAT/GRE — required; competitive scores ~680+
  • Essays —
  • One-page CV, two references, English test (waived if prior education was in English).
  • Interview — by invitation, largely conducted by alumni. Anfisa Bashkirova, who leads our executive practice, is among the Saïd alumni involved in admissions — we prepare candidates for the format from direct knowledge of it.
  • Rounds — several deadlines across the autumn–spring cycle

Cost and funding

  • Tuition:
  • Scholarships: Saïd runs several named awards — Skoll (social entrepreneurship), women's scholarships, regional awards — some automatic, some by separate essay. For international candidates this is the principal lever.

What this looks like in practice

Over the past year 10+ ALBION clients completed Saïd programmes — the largest single-school track in our executive practice. We know the interview format, the essay expectations and the scholarship landscape from the inside.

Questions

Is one year enough?+

For career acceleration — yes, that is the design. For a full industry pivot, the compression is real: the decision needs an honest conversation.

Oxford MBA or EMBA?+

MBA — full-time, career acceleration. EMBA — modular, for senior managers staying in role. EMBA →

What if my GMAT is modest?+

The score is one input; a strong career narrative and the right round choice change the mathematics. Waivers are assessed individually.
