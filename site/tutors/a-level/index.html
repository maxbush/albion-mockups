Tutors for the British Curriculum: GCSE, A-Level, IB

A-Level Tutors

A-Levels are the grades that decide university offers: a conditional offer reads &quot;A*AA&quot; — three letters that open or close a corridor. Preparation here is not &quot;improve the subject&quot; but &quot;hit the grade boundary the offer requires&quot;.

Book a consultation

Subject choice is half the strategy

A-Level choices are made in Year 11 and lock the university map:

  • medicine

    chemistry is non-negotiable, plus biology;

  • engineering, economics at top departments

    maths and often further maths;

  • humanities

    "facilitating subjects" keep options open.

A wrong combination can quietly close Oxbridge courses two years before the application. We check subject sets against the actual university route — before the choices are filed.

How tutoring works

  • Subject specialists per A-Level

    the exam boards (AQA, Edexcel, OCR, CIE) differ; we match to the board.

  • Mark-scheme literacy

    top grades come from knowing what examiners reward, not only knowing the subject.

  • Predicted grades

    schools predict from Year 12 performance; early work pays twice, in the prediction and the final.

  • Retake strategy

    where a first attempt underdelivered, retakes are planned carefully: some universities view them coldly, others neutrally. The answer is course-specific.

Rates

From £65/hour; admissions-test-level preparation £95–150/hour.

Questions

A-Level or IB?+

A-Level for depth and a known direction; IB for breadth and international portability. IB → — and we can compare both routes for your case.

Preparation for international pupils abroad?+

Yes — online, with the exam sat at a British school or international centre.
