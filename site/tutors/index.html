Tutors for the British Curriculum: GCSE, A-Level, IB

More than 150 tutors work with ALBION — selected and coordinated by a dedicated specialist, not drawn from a marketplace. Most are UK-qualified teachers and examiners; several teach within the Oxford system.

What tutoring at ALBION is

  • A team, not a directory. We match the tutor to the brief — the subject, the exam board, the child's temperament — and the consultant keeps the whole picture: tutoring, admissions and guardianship in one hand.
  • Goal-driven, not open-ended. Sessions serve a named objective: an entry exam, a target grade, a scholarship paper. Progress is measured against the brief.
  • Aligned with the admissions route. For pupils heading to British schools and universities, the tutor sees the admissions requirements — subject choices, boards, formats — rather than teaching "in general".

By programme

  • GCSE → — the foundation of the British system; the results set the sixth-form corridor.
  • A-Level → — three or four subjects chosen for the university route; grades decide offers.
  • IB → — the broader diploma; different demands, different preparation.
  • Revision courses → — intensive pre-exam programmes, including Easter revision.
  • (/tutors/homeschooling/) — full-curriculum teaching outside school.

Rates

  • Subject tutor — from £65/hour.
  • Entrance-exam and admissions specialist — £85–110/hour.
  • Senior specialist (university admissions tests, Oxbridge preparation) — £95–150/hour.

The number of hours follows the assessment — we name an honest figure rather than sell a package.

What this looks like in practice

A whole GCSE programme from scratch — anonymised. A home-schooled pupil, a tournament chess player, joined with English well below school level and suspected dyslexia. ALBION tutors delivered the full GCSE programme; he went on to an offer from a strong school for A-Level at 14.

Twelve grades in a term — anonymised. A pupil arrived mid-course with grades slipping across subjects. A coordinated tutor team — not one teacher but a managed plan — brought the report card to the level the target sixth form required within a term.
